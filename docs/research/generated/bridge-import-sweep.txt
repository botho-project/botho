### Self-churn adversary (no domestic money)

The coin is spent to its owner every 12 blocks. Nothing domestic enters. Under the default wallet the 5% decay alone strips the import tag, and `ClusterTagVector` prunes it to background below 0.1%. The max-shed spender declares decay 95.0% per hop (weight ÷ 20), the most it can shed while its claimed wealth stays at the ring-centroid floor. Since protocol 10.0.0 consensus keeps at least 95% of each cluster's weight per spend wherever every spend candidate carries it: the default wallet's 5% decay is at that floor and passes, the max-shed spend is rejected at its first hop, and only a spender whose 19 decoys share the coin's exact amount escapes the floor (it still pays the import floor read from the inputs, which scales with the import share they still carry, so about 1x after one shed).

### Tag-drop adversary (background outputs)

First spend after one day, honest wallet vs background outputs (protocol 10.0.0, #1687). With decoys of other amounts the drop is rejected (`drop accepted` = no): the coin is its input's only spend candidate, so the retained-weight floor binds. With 19 background decoys of the coin's exact amount the floor's minimum is zero and the drop is accepted, but the import floor is read from the inputs (the candidates' maximum import share), so the drop is charged at F or more and pays the capitalized downgrade charge on the gap below it.

| coin | honest factor | honest fee | drop accepted | same-amount drop factor | its import floor | its ring floor | its fee |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| small entrant (1,000 BTH alone) | 1.500x | 0.4920% | no | 1.500x | 1.500x | 1.265x | 1.0000% |
| flood coin (10,000 of 2,000,000) | 5.448x | 0.0220% | no | 5.448x | 1.500x | 5.448x | 8.8960% |
| undiluted whale (2,000,000 alone) | 5.448x | 0.0220% | no | 5.448x | 1.500x | 5.448x | 8.8960% |
